Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Version 1.0

Short version: Full refund within 20 days of purchase if the software has not been deployed to a production environment. After deployment, no refunds are available.

1. Refund Window

You may request a full refund within 20 days of your purchase date. After 20 days, no refunds will be issued.

2. Eligibility

  • Request made within 20 days of purchase
  • Software has not been deployed to any public-facing production environment
  • You provide a reason for the request
  • Purchase was made through an authorized channel (Lemon Squeezy or similar)

3. When Refunds Are NOT Available

  • The 20-day window has passed
  • The software has been deployed to production
  • You have already generated revenue from your installation
  • You modified the code significantly and now want a refund because the modifications broke something
  • You purchased by mistake and waited more than 20 days to tell us

4. How to Request a Refund

Email business_obs@protonmail.com with:

  • Purchase email address
  • Order ID or transaction reference
  • Product name and version
  • Reason for the request
  • Confirmation the software has not been deployed to production

5. Process & Timeline

  1. You send the request to business_obs@protonmail.com
  2. We respond within 48 hours
  3. If eligible, we authorize the refund
  4. Lemon Squeezy (our merchant of record) processes the refund
  5. Funds arrive in 5–10 business days, depending on your payment method

6. Partial Refunds

Partial refunds are not offered. A refund is either the full price or nothing.

7. Disputes

If you disagree with a refund decision, you can escalate through the marketplace that handled the transaction. We cooperate fully with any marketplace-mediated dispute.

8. Exchanges

We do not offer exchanges between products. Request a refund on the original (if eligible) and purchase the other product separately.

9. Chargebacks

Please contact us before initiating a chargeback. Chargebacks filed without prior contact may result in permanent revocation of your license.

10. Support Window vs. Refund Window

The support window (6 months) and the refund window (20 days) are independent. A refund after the support window is impossible; a support request after the refund window is still valid within 6 months.
